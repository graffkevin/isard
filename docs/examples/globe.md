# The globe

The IGN's orthophotos (Géoplateforme) on a sphere among the stars, turning slowly until the map is touched. A click flies to the point
along van Wijk and Nuij's path (MapLibre's `flyTo`), the sphere blending into Web Mercator between zooms 5 and 7 (later under a tilted camera).

The same in [React](./react/globe).
