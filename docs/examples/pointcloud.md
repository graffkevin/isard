# Point clouds

Notre-Dame de Paris and the Île de la Cité in the IGN's LiDAR HD (a tile of 1 km², 15.5 million points, Licence
Ouverte): its COPC file read by range requests on the Géoplateforme, the nodes in view only, decoded in the workers,
over the orthophotos and the IGN relief. Colored by class, altitude or intensity, with Eye-Dome Lighting; the point
under the cursor is picked. See [point clouds](../layers/pointclouds).
