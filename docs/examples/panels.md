# 3D panels

Elements of the page standing in 3D on the forecourt of the Sacré-Cœur (`Panel`): a photo 40 metres wide and a
sign; any element stands this way, a video or an iframe as well. Markers raised 30 and 60 metres above the ground,
their stem down to it (`Marker`'s `height`). Turn the map around them.
