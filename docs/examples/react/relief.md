# Relief and a ground shader

The IGN relief (RGE ALTI, `terrain: { model: 'ign' }`) and PLAN.IGN under the Mont Blanc with a sky and fog, and a fragment shader of the page drawn over the ground: rings every 500 m around the cursor, two pixels wide at any zoom, lying on the mountains like the basemap.

In React (`isard/react`); the same in [TypeScript](../relief).
