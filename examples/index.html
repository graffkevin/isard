Isard examples

  • The globe: turning in its atmosphere, a flight to a click
  • A map: the vector basemap, controls, a marker and its popup
  • Travel times on hover: a network recolored at every move
  • GeoJSON layers: fills by value, outlines, labels, picking
  • Buildings: raised to their height, colored by it
  • Relief and a ground shader: GLSL of the page over the mountains
  • 3D Tiles: the Lille photomesh
  • glTF models: wind turbines on the relief, textured trucks, picked
  • Point clouds: Notre-Dame de Paris in the IGN's LiDAR HD (COPC), picked
  • Viewshed: what can be seen from a point over the relief and the buildings (prototype)
  • Viewshed on a mesh: the same over the textured mesh of Strasbourg (3D Tiles)
  • three.js in the map: a custom layer sharing the depth of the buildings
  • Vector basemaps: OpenFreeMap's styles, a restyled subset
  • Lambert-93 data: PLAN IGN reprojected from Lambert-93, a WFS in EPSG:2154, a GPX file
  • The Moon: another body, the LRO mosaic, the Apollo sites
  • Mars: its thin dusty halo, the Viking mosaic, famous sites
